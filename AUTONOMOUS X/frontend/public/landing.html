Who We Are

Autonomous X is a full-service logistics network built on ground expertise and modern technology. From our hubs in Dubai, Chennai and Singapore, we move freight by air, sea, road and rail — and coordinate the people, warehouses and vehicles that keep global trade flowing. We exist to make shipping effortless for our customers and profitable for our partners, on one connected platform.
